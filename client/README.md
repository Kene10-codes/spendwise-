# SpendWise 

## Spendwise is an application that allows users to manage their personal finances in one place.

Imagine a user receives their salary, pays rent, buys groceries, pays for transportation, and earns additional income. SpendWise will help them understand:
- How much money they have earned.
- How much they have spent.
- Which categories consume most of their money.
- Whether they are staying within their budget.
- How their finances change from month to month.

The app should be useful on desktop and mobile devices.
